# LyricGuess — Personas

These are lightweight, assumption-based personas built from the target audience
in the brief. They are NOT derived from user research yet — they're the
hypotheses we'll test in `research-plan.md`. Update with real findings after
testing.

## Primary — "Between-class Maya"
- College sophomore, 19. Phone-first, always has earbuds in.
- Plays casual games in short gaps: waiting for class, in line, on the bus.
- Loves music but isn't a trivia hardcore. Wants fun, not a challenge that
  makes her feel dumb.
- Success for her: a quick, satisfying round she can beat her own score on and
  screenshot to friends.
- Friction she won't tolerate: sign-up walls, long intros, slow loads.

## Secondary — "Competitive Deb"
- 22, recent grad. Plays daily-puzzle games (Wordle, Connections) and shares
  scores in group chats.
- Motivated by streaks and beating past scores.
- Would be the first to want a leaderboard — a signal for the roadmap, not V1.

## Anti-persona — "Music-nerd Theo"
- Wants obscure deep cuts, full lyrics, granular genres, artist rabbit holes.
- We are deliberately NOT optimizing for him in V1. If we chase depth we lose
  the casual, low-friction feel that's the whole point.
