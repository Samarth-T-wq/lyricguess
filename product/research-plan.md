# LyricGuess — Research Plan

This is a PLAN for user testing, not a record of completed research. Nothing here
has been run yet. Fill in findings after sessions.

## Goal
Validate the core assumptions behind V1 before over-investing:
1. Is the setup (genre → difficulty → mode) low-friction, or too many taps?
2. Is the core round loop fun and clear?
3. Does feedback feel satisfying enough to prompt "play again"?
4. Are the difficulty tiers calibrated right?

## Method (lightweight, honest, doable)
- **Moderated hallway testing:** 5–8 people from the target audience (college
  students / younger listeners). Watch them play one full session.
- **Think-aloud:** ask them to narrate what they're doing and expecting.
- **Post-session questions:** what was confusing? would you play again? what
  felt good/bad?

## What to watch
- Where do they hesitate during setup?
- Do they understand the score/streak without explanation?
- Do they reach for Play Again unprompted?
- Any point they wanted to quit?

## Success signal
Most testers finish a session, understand it without help, and voluntarily
replay at least once.

## After testing
Log findings in `decision-log.md` and update `personas.md` with real
observations. Do not present testing hypotheses as results.
