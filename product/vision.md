# LyricGuess — Vision

## One-liner
A fast, mobile-first lyric-guessing game you can play in two minutes between classes.

## The problem
Casual music trivia is either buried inside heavy apps (Spotify quizzes, music
apps with accounts and setup) or lives on clunky web pages. There's no clean,
Wordle-simple "read a lyric, guess the song" loop that respects your time.

## The vision
LyricGuess is the quick-hit music trivia game for younger listeners. Open it,
pick a vibe, play ten fast rounds, see how you did, play again. No account, no
tutorial, no friction. It should feel modern and satisfying — the kind of thing
you screenshot your score from and send to a friend.

## Why now / why us
Free lyric and metadata APIs (Lyrics.ovh, MusicBrainz) make a lightweight
version buildable without licensing a full catalog, as long as we stay in
short-snippet, fair-use territory. That keeps V1 cheap and fast to ship.

## What success looks like (V1)
People finish sessions and replay. If the core loop is fun enough that a player
taps "Play Again," we've validated the concept and earned the right to build
more (social, leaderboards, new modes).

## What this is NOT
Not a full music app. Not a social network. Not a hardcore music-nerd quiz.
It's a light, replayable trivia snack.
