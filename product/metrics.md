# LyricGuess — Success Metrics

Targets below are goals to evaluate against once the game is live and
instrumented. They are NOT current results — no data has been collected yet.
Fill in actuals after launch/testing.

## North Star
**Session completion rate** — % of started sessions that reach the results
screen. This is the cleanest signal that the core loop is fun.

## Supporting metrics
- **Replay rate** — % of completed sessions where the player taps Play Again.
- **Rounds completed per session** — are people dropping mid-session, and where?
- **Accuracy curve by difficulty** — is Easy too easy, Hard too hard?
- **Genre/mode mix** — what do people actually pick?

## Guardrail / quality signals
- **Error-shown rate** — how often players hit an error state.
- **Time-to-first-round** — proxy for setup friction.

## Targets (hypotheses to validate, not results)
- Session completion: aim for >60% once tuned.
- Replay rate: aim for >30%.
- Error rate: keep <5% of sessions.

These come directly from the analytics events in brief §10.
